## 2026-09-08 - Fix CSV Injection vulnerability in ReservationExporter
**Vulnerability:** The CSV exporter (`ReservationExporter`) manually constructs CSV files and has logic to prepend a single quote (`'`) to fields starting with formula trigger characters (`=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, `\t`). To handle leading whitespace appropriately (which is ignored by spreadsheets), it uses `isSpreadsheetTrimmable` to skip past it. However, it was treating `\r` (carriage return) as ordinary trimmable whitespace. This meant a payload like `\r=cmd` would skip `\r`, see `=`, and escape it, BUT a payload like `\rcmd` would skip `\r`, see `c` (not a trigger), not escape it, and leave `\r` at the start. Since `\r` is a formula trigger character in spreadsheets, this allowed an injection payload to bypass the protection. Moreover, the first non-whitespace character switch did not include `\r`.
**Learning:** When manually writing CSV Formula Injection protections, all characters that act as formula triggers in spreadsheet programs (such as `\t` and `\r`) must be explicitly excluded from whitespace trimming functions, AND they must be explicitly included in the check for formula triggers.
**Prevention:** Thoroughly review what characters spreadsheet programs treat as formula triggers or ignore as whitespace, and ensure trimming/skipping logic excludes ALL formula trigger characters (like `\r` and `\t`). Write specific tests for payloads starting directly with obscure trigger characters followed by non-trigger characters (e.g. `\rcmd`).
